The model writes a story with your words. It weaves them in by itself; the board gives a growing push to the words not used yet (logit bias) and finishes a started word letter by letter (FSM). Only English words work: the model learned on English stories.
The model continues your text. No words are pushed here: this is the model on its own.
micro-qwen3: the Qwen3 architecture, scaled down about 560 times. Trained on TinyStories.
Sampling: top-k, then min-p 0.02, with a repetition penalty.
